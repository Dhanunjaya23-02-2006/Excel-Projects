# 📊 Superstore Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

An interactive retail sales analytics dashboard built entirely in Microsoft Excel using the Sample Superstore dataset.

The project analyzes sales, profit, orders, customers, products, regions, segments, shipping methods, and time trends.

## Business Objective

Help sales managers:

- Monitor revenue and profitability
- Identify high-performing products and customers
- Compare regional performance
- Understand category and segment performance
- Identify loss-making products
- Analyze sales trends over time
- Support data-driven marketing and sales decisions

## Dashboard KPIs

1. Total Sales
2. Total Profit
3. Total Orders
4. Average Order Value
5. Profit Margin %

### KPI Definitions

```text
AOV = Total Sales ÷ Unique Orders

Profit Margin % = Total Profit ÷ Total Sales × 100
```
