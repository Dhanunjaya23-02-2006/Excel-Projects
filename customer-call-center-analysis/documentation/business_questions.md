# Business Questions

This project analyzes customer call center performance using Microsoft Excel,
Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, slicers, and data visualization.

The analysis focuses on the following business questions.

## 1. How many calls are we getting by customer?

**Analysis Goal:**  
Identify customers with the highest and lowest number of calls.

**Excel Concepts Used:**
- Pivoting Data
- Count of Calls
- Customer-level Analysis

**Business Value:**  
Helps identify customers who frequently contact the call center.

---

## 2. How satisfied are our customers?

**Analysis Goal:**  
Analyze the distribution and average of customer satisfaction ratings.

**Excel Concepts Used:**
- Pivot Table Calculations
- Average Satisfaction Rating
- Changing Calculation Mode

**Business Value:**  
Helps measure overall customer satisfaction and identify areas that require improvement.

---

## 3. Who are our top 10 customers?

**Analysis Goal:**  
Identify the top 10 customers based on purchase amount.

**Excel Concepts Used:**
- Pivot Table Sorting
- Top 10 Filters
- Ranking

**Business Value:**  
Helps identify the most valuable customers.

---

## 4. Who are the top 10 customers for a specific representative?

**Analysis Goal:**  
Identify the highest-value customers handled by a selected representative.

**Excel Concepts Used:**
- Report Filters
- Slicers
- Top 10 Analysis

**Business Value:**  
Allows representative-level customer performance analysis.

---

## 5. Call duration analysis

**Analysis Goal:**  
Analyze the distribution of call durations.

**Excel Concepts Used:**
- Grouping
- Bucketing

**Business Value:**  
Helps understand whether calls are generally short, medium, or long.

---

## 6. How busy is our call center in 2023?

**Analysis Goal:**  
Analyze call volume trends throughout the year.

**Excel Concepts Used:**
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Trend Analysis

**Business Value:**  
Helps identify busy and low-volume periods.

---

## 7. Year-to-Date Sales Analysis

**Analysis Goal:**  
Analyze cumulative sales performance throughout the year.

**Excel Concepts Used:**
- Value Field Settings
- Running Total
- YTD Analysis

**Business Value:**  
Helps track sales growth over time.

---

## 8. Which days of the week are the busiest?

**Analysis Goal:**  
Identify the days with the highest customer call volume.

**Excel Concepts Used:**
- Pivot Tables
- Conditional Formatting

**Business Value:**  
Helps optimize staffing and resource allocation.

---

## 9. Is there a relationship between call duration and customer satisfaction rating?

**Analysis Goal:**  
Determine whether longer or shorter calls are associated with customer satisfaction.

**Excel Concepts Used:**
- Calculated Columns
- Refreshing Data
- Scatter Plot

**Business Value:**  
Helps understand whether call duration affects customer experience.

---

## 10. Should we hire extra people in any specific months?

**Analysis Goal:**  
Identify months with unusually high call volumes.

**Excel Concepts Used:**
- Monthly Trend Analysis
- Conditional Formatting

**Business Value:**  
Supports workforce planning and staffing decisions.

---

## 11. Time Wasters: Which customers call the most but purchase the least?

**Analysis Goal:**  
Identify customers with high call frequency but low purchase amounts.

**Business Value:**  
Helps identify potentially inefficient customer interactions and opportunities
to improve call handling strategies.

---

## 12. Rep Training: Which representatives could benefit from satisfaction training?

**Analysis Goal:**  
Identify representatives with lower customer satisfaction ratings.

**Business Value:**  
Helps management target training programs toward representatives who may
need additional customer service support.
