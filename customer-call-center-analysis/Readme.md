## 🔍 Business Questions

The analysis answers the following questions:

1. How many calls are received from each customer?
2. How satisfied are customers?
3. Who are the top 10 customers by purchase amount?
4. Who are the top 10 customers for a selected representative?
5. What is the distribution of call durations?
6. How busy was the call center throughout 2023?
7. What is the Year-to-Date sales trend?
8. Which days of the week are the busiest?
9. Is there a relationship between call duration and customer satisfaction?
10. During which months should additional staff be considered?
11. Which customers call the most but purchase the least?
12. Which representatives could benefit from customer satisfaction training?
